01 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Begin with who will use the machine

A staff room and a student-accessible space have different needs. Identify the intended users, approximate daily demand and permitted access times before considering equipment or products. School administrators should guide the discussion about what is appropriate for each location.

02 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Make policies part of product planning

Share district requirements and any applicable nutrition or food policies at the outset. Proposed selections need to be reviewed against the rules that apply to the school and the intended use. A general vending assortment should not be assumed to meet every school’s requirements.

03 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Plan placement and access thoughtfully

Discuss supervision, circulation, electrical access and delivery routes with the facilities team. School calendars and approved service windows can also affect coordination. Confirm the proposed placement and service arrangements before proceeding.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your school vending questions.

Can we discuss vending for a staff room?

Yes. Explain the intended users, approximate usage and available space so that staff-area options can be considered.

Can the school review the product selection?

Product preferences and school policies should be part of the consultation. The responsible school team should review proposed selections before agreement.

What does the school need to provide first?

Prepare the school’s location, intended users, estimated daily usage, proposed placement and relevant policies or access restrictions.
