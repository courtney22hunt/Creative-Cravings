About Creative Cravings

Vending that fits
your location

Creative Cravings is a New Jersey–based business providing vending machines for commercial buildings, hospitals, doctors’ offices and schools. We focus on making snacks and beverages convenient to access—and making your experience with us straightforward and personal.

Every building serves different people. Employees may need a snack between meetings. Hospital staff and visitors may want a drink during a long day. Doctors’ offices and schools have their own space requirements, routines and product preferences.

We start by listening. Understanding who will use your machines, where they will go and what your facility needs helps guide our recommendations. From machine formats to product selections, we work with you to find an appropriate fit.
