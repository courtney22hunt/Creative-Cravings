01 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

One refreshment point for different cravings

Combination vending brings snack and beverage choices into one format. It can be useful to explore for a workplace or shared facility where people want both types of products. The right choice still depends on the assortment and capacity your location needs.

02 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Balance floor space with demand

A single machine format does not automatically suit every small space. Review the proposed equipment dimensions, electrical needs, delivery access and clearance. Compare expected demand for snacks and drinks before deciding whether a combination unit or separate machines makes more sense.

03 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Choose a mix that reflects everyday use

Discuss the relative interest in drinks and snacks, preferred products and any site policies. Those details help guide a proposed assortment. Restocking, payment capabilities and machine care should be confirmed for the equipment and service plan being considered.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your combination vending questions.

Is combination vending suitable for an office?

It is a format worth discussing when both snack and drink choices are wanted. Suitability depends on user numbers, space and equipment availability.

How does it compare with separate machines?

Separate machines and combination formats offer different capacity and selection possibilities. Compare both options against the needs of the location.

What information helps with choosing a machine?

Prepare your location, estimated daily users, available space, preferred products and desired timeline.
