01 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Different spaces serve different people

A hospital staff area, a medical office waiting room and a shared medical building have different patterns of use. Identify who the machine is for and where refreshment access would be most helpful. Patient-facing selections and placement should be reviewed with the facility’s responsible team.

02 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Work within facility requirements

Discuss approved placement areas, visitor circulation, delivery access, cleaning expectations and any food or beverage policies. Equipment and product decisions should follow the facility’s requirements rather than assuming that every waiting area is appropriate for vending.

03 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Coordinate service with the building

Healthcare environments may have controlled access or specific delivery windows. Explain these arrangements during consultation, along with a designated facility contact. Restocking and machine care can then be discussed in the context of the building’s routines.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your healthcare vending questions.

Do you discuss vending for both hospitals and doctors’ offices?

Yes. Convenient Cravings works with these facility types to explore suitable vending options. Proposed arrangements depend on the individual location and requirements.

Can products reflect our facility’s food policies?

Share the policies and selection requirements during consultation. The proposed assortment should be reviewed with the appropriate facility representative.

Can a machine be placed in a staff-only area?

Staff-only placement can be discussed. Access, available space, equipment requirements and service coordination need to be considered.
