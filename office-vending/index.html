01 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

A better break for your workplace

A small office, a shared building and a busy workplace can have very different refreshment needs. Start with the number of people who regularly use the space, when they take breaks and whether visitors or tenants will also use the machines. These details help guide the choice between snack, beverage and combination formats.

02 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Plan around the space you have

A breakroom, lobby or shared amenity area may offer a useful placement opportunity. Before choosing a machine, discuss the available floor space, nearby electrical supply, delivery access and room for people to use the machine comfortably. A site discussion helps identify practical requirements before placement.

03 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Build a service plan around your people

Share the snacks and drinks your team prefers, any workplace food policies and the times when access is busiest. Product selection, restocking arrangements and machine care should be discussed together so that the proposed plan reflects how your building operates.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your office vending questions.

Can a small office have a vending machine?

Suitability depends on expected usage, available space and the proposed service arrangement. Share your approximate daily user count and location so those factors can be discussed.

Can one machine offer both snacks and drinks?

A combination machine is one format to consider when a shared snack-and-beverage selection is appropriate. The choice depends on space, demand and available equipment.

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Have your New Jersey city or ZIP code, approximate daily users, preferred location, product interests and desired timeline ready.
