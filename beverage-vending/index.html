01 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Match the drink selection to the setting

Start with the beverages your users ask for, from water to everyday favorites. A staff breakroom and a public waiting area may need different assortments. Share preferences and facility policies so that the proposed selection has a clear purpose.

02 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Review demand before choosing a format

Expected daily users, peak periods and the balance between staff and visitor use help inform capacity discussions. Space, electrical needs and access for delivery and servicing should also be reviewed before a machine is selected.

03 / PLAN YOUR SERVICE

Keep the service conversation practical

Discuss how restocking would be coordinated and who would be the facility contact for product feedback or machine issues. Confirm available equipment, payment options and service arrangements during consultation rather than assuming every format has the same features.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your beverage vending questions.

Can we request water and other drink options?

Share your preferred mix during consultation. The final selection depends on the machine format, product availability and facility policies.

Will a beverage machine fit in our waiting area?

That depends on the available space, access and facility requirements. Provide a description or measurements of the proposed location for discussion.

Are cashless payments available?

Payment capabilities depend on the equipment proposed. Confirm supported payment methods before agreeing to a machine or placement.
